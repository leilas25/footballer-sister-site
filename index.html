Life Behind the Game

What Is The Footballer's Sister?

The Footballer’s Sister is more than just a football platform — it’s a look into everything that happens behind the game.

It’s about the support, the lifestyle, the behind-the-scenes moments and sometimes the very real moments that come with being part of a football journey.

Through this platform, we document the journeys of the players, celebrate their progress, share the moments that matter and grow alongside them. From matchdays and family life to the challenges, milestones and memories along the way, The Footballer’s Sister is a place to tell the story of football beyond the pitch.
